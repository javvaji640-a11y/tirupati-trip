Simple Day-wise Plan

Day 1Travel from Hyderabad to Tirupati by bus and check in to the hotel.
Day 2Start from Tirupati, walk to Tirumala, complete darshan, return to Tirupati and rest.
Day 3Take a rental car, visit Kanipakam and continue to Arunachalam. Check in to the hotel.
Day 4Complete Arunachalam visit and Girivalam, then travel to Srikalahasti.
Day 5Complete Srikalahasti pooja, return to Tirupati Railway Station and board the train home.
ImportantKeep darshan, hotel, rental car and train details ready before the journey.
