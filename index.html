Simple day-wise plan

Day 1Travel from Hyderabad to Tirupati by overnight bus and check in to the hotel.
Day 2Walk from Alipiri to Tirumala, complete darshan, return to Tirupati and rest.
Day 3Take the rental car, visit Kanipakam and continue to Arunachalam. Check in to the hotel.
Day 4Complete the Arunachalam temple visit and Girivalam, then travel to Srikalahasti.
Day 5Complete Srikalahasti pooja, return to Tirupati Railway Station and board the train home.
Before leavingKeep darshan tickets, hotel bookings, rental car and train details saved offline.
